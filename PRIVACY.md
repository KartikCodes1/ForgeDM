# ForgeDM privacy policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

ForgeDM is a download manager for Windows, with an optional browser
extension. This policy covers both.

## The short version

ForgeDM doesn't collect, sell or share any information about you. It has no
accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry, and nothing you do in ForgeDM is sent
to us.

## What stays on your PC

ForgeDM keeps these on your computer only:

- your settings, save rules, queues and schedules
- your download list and history, including the links you downloaded and
  the folders you saved to
- download statistics shown on the dashboard

You can delete all of it by removing ForgeDM. The Microsoft Store version
removes this data when you uninstall it. The installer version asks whether
to delete it. Downloaded files are never deleted when you uninstall.

## What ForgeDM connects to

- **The websites you download from.** ForgeDM connects to the servers
  hosting the files you download, and sends them what a web browser would
  send, such as the cookies and referring page that came with a link from
  your browser, so the download works the same way it would in the browser.
- **Nothing else.** ForgeDM doesn't contact any server of ours.

## The browser extension

The extension watches for downloads and video streams on the pages you
visit, so it can offer to download them with ForgeDM. When you download
something, it sends the link, the page it came from and that site's cookies
to the ForgeDM app on your own computer (at 127.0.0.1). This information
never leaves your PC except when ForgeDM connects to that same site to
download the file. To list the qualities of a video, the extension may load
the video's playlist from the site you're on.

The extension only talks to ForgeDM after you approve it in the app, and you
can disconnect it in ForgeDM's settings at any time.

## Virus scanning

If you turn on "Scan with Microsoft Defender", ForgeDM asks Microsoft Defender on your PC
to scan finished files. Defender's own settings decide whether it shares
anything with Microsoft.

## Children

ForgeDM doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children.
